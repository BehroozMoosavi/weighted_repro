\caption{Joint coverage for $(\mu,\sigma)$ as the true mean approaches the
upper clamp $U=3$, where half the latent distribution lies above the upper
bound. Rows are $\mu^\ast$ and columns are the GDP parameter
$\mu_{\mathrm{GDP}}$ per coordinate; the released pair satisfies
$\sqrt{2}\mu_{\mathrm{GDP}}$-GDP. Mahalanobis and Penalized Wald approximate
nominal $95\%$ joint regions, with lower bound $0.95025$ for the exact rank
rules. PB--ADI instead uses a rectangle of two nominal $95\%$ marginal
intervals; valid marginal calibration would imply a joint lower bound of
$0.90$, not an exact joint level. The grey scale breaks at $0.95$, so light
cells indicate observed coverage below that reference, not by themselves
population undercoverage. Each cell has $100$ attempted replications:
failed repro constructions count as non-coverage, whereas failed PB--ADI
fits are excluded from its averages. The Monte Carlo standard error near
coverage $0.95$ is about $0.022$ when all $100$ replications contribute.}
